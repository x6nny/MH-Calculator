How it's calculated: Total no resets = product of every multiplier (× qty). Total = Total no resetsresetters + 1, one full pass per reset. Total Length = Σ length × qty + extra length (one pass). How it's calculated: each rebirth gives skips + 1 lives (20 skips = 21 lives). Rebirths per hour = 3600 ÷ speed, and lives per hour = rebirths per hour × lives per rebirth. Every skip multiplies the cash you need by 1,000, so 20 skips need price × 1060. When the price field is blank, it is estimated from the life ranges in the MPU site's suffix list: the price climbs smoothly through each suffix's life range and is capped at 10 NVSPTGNTL (10241) from life 80,351. Time to target assumes every rebirth takes the same number of seconds.

How it's calculated: each purchase raises the price of the next platform of that type. With k already owned, the next price is Start + (+ per buy) × min(k, first) + (then +) × max(0, k − first), and the cost of buying several is the sum of those prices. Small Platforms are sold by The Masked Man for a random 117–221 uC and don't scale. Shards per rebirth defaults to ⌊skips ÷ 2⌋ using the Rebirth tab's skips (1 Shard of Life per 2 lives skipped), and the time to earn the shards uses that tab's speed.

How it builds: cash needed = rebirth price × 1,000skips, and ore value = ore base value × furnace × resetter multipliers × setup multiplier (the MPU site's model). The builder only uses items you've marked as owned, never uses one item more times than you have copies, and tries every number of your resetters (strongest first). Each normal item counts once per pass (resetters + 1 passes), Preline states count once, and N Resetters states already include every pass so they're only used when exactly N resetters are used. It picks the best multiplier-per-length items first, then drops any item the target doesn't need, and shows the shortest setup that hits your skips. Resetter lengths aren't counted. Many items have conditions (wet ore, Havium, Midas only, other items required); check the Conditions column before you build.
